# Hyperliquid–Binance matched perpetual funding carry

Compare long and short positions of equal native units across Hyperliquid and Binance, where funding rates can differ even when the contracts track the same underlying. Binance launched BTCUSDC and ETHUSDC perpetuals in January 2024. Both settle in USDC. Hyperliquid BTC and ETH perpetuals settle in USDC and use USDT-denominated oracle indices, so the hedge retains a venue-specific basis and settlement-index risk. [Binance USDC contract launch](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/4ff3534898ab4ea09afb450a94f20afe) · [Hyperliquid contract specification](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/contract-specifications) · [Independent research on perp pricing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888)

## Frozen protocol

- Use 12 consecutive 28-day windows from December 21, 2025 through November 22, 2026 UTC. Use the first ten as historical diagnostics and the last two consecutive windows, September 27–October 25 and October 25–November 22, as primary tests of the combined spread. Hyperliquid data overlap earlier research. We froze the joint rules before viewing joint returns and used only the data availability and schema for the initial Binance probe.
- Start with 1,000 USDC on each venue. Allocate equal BTC and ETH sleeves. Use 45% of a whole sleeve's capital as the target matched notional on each venue, reducing size if the smaller independent venue balance cannot support it. Round down to the common 0.001 BTC/ETH lot, and apply both venues' minimum order amounts. Reinvest from the independently settled venue balances and keep the surplus on its original venue. Keep idle balances in USDC without interest. Require separately funded initial margin at each venue and a 5% reserve on marked notional even when the actual maintenance tier is lower.
- At 00:00 UTC on the vintage start, consider both directions. Sum each venue's past funding into four disjoint seven-day intervals. Take four times the median of the four summed paired cash flows, crediting 75% of favorable settlements and charging 100% of unfavorable settlements. Value the Hyperliquid credits at 0.5% lower and the debits at 0.5% higher oracle price relative to matched notional. Use strictly past settlement hours. Require absolute basis within 15 bps at the prior completed close and at least 100,000 USDC of median four-hour traded notional per leg. Select the direction with highest projected net funding only if it exceeds the cost and opportunity hurdle. Subtract 19 bps for both taker round trips, 40 bps for four fills at 10 bps each, and a 15 bp adverse basis allowance, and require the resulting sleeve return to exceed compounded 4% cash. Keep the unused asset sleeve idle.
- Use the next four-hour open for both modeled entries and close both at the end of each 28-day window. Pay all four fills per vintage. Use the observed exchange trade price opens to model synchronized basis at execution. These bars cannot verify the fill or the interval between legs. Use 2 bps per fill for base and 10 bps per fill with the positive-credit stress for the qualifying replay. Charge 4.5 bps on Hyperliquid and apply a 5 bp Binance VIP 0 taker assumption without discounts. Use historical Binance settlement marks and signed per-settlement funding. The Hyperliquid historical oracle is unavailable on this tape, so use archived Hyperliquid spot bar opens and stress via the low for credits and high for debits, with an additional 0.5% adverse price allowance. Omit positive funding at entry and exit hours and charge negative funding at both boundaries. Verify entry/exit units are within 1% of both four-hour volumes.
- Compare with the same independently funded equal-weight backtest that always takes the direction paid most favorably in the four observed prior funding weeks. Apply the same basis and liquidity screens to both. Compare both to idle cash and the 4% compounded comparator. Count a qualifying vintage only if it beats the 4% compounded comparator under full stress and all modeled positions pass the capacity and funding checks. As a separate tail sensitivity, add 100 bps to the adverse exit of one venue and report its effect. The results are paper estimates with counterparty, USDC and broken-hedge risk.

## Results

The selective spread placed zero trades. The strongest stress-adjusted projection was **47.29 bps**, from short Hyperliquid ETH on December 21, 2025. The frozen entry hurdle was **140.96 bps**. All 24 pair decisions met the trailing basis and liquidity screens. The holdout periods' strongest projected spread was just 19.06 bps in September–October and 11.74 bps in October–November. Idle USDC ended at **2,000.00** versus **2,073.53** for the compounded cash comparator.

Amounts are USDC, with fees and execution listed as charges. Neither cost scenario cleared the 4% cash hurdle in any window. Under stress, the realized receipt haircut consumed most of the small positive funding spread before taker costs. Closing a single leg with an additional 100 bp execution charge would change the final two stressed net results to **−8.58** and **−6.66** USDC. The two venues held separate cash ledgers throughout. All modeled positions met both venues' minimum order size, had sufficient initial funding, and stayed below 1% of four-hour volume. For every modeled short and long, the minimum four-hour stressed margin headroom was at least **286.35 USDC**.

Binance announced a **4 bp** regular-user USDC perpetual taker fee effective December 10, 2025, with the March 2026 update extending the promotion until further notice. Replaying the complete process at that fee lowered the entry hurdle to **138.96 bps**. The selective spread still stayed idle. The direction benchmark ended at **1,990.58 USDC** with the base execution allowance and **1,948.96 USDC** under stress. Its final two stressed vintages lost **4.37** and **3.72** USDC. ## Data and reproduction

Coverage spans November 23, 2025 through November 22, 2026 UTC, with 2,185 trade candles per asset and venue, 8,737 hourly Hyperliquid settlements per asset, and 1,093 Binance settlements per asset. Binance settlements use recorded mark prices. The Binance margin calculation uses separate historical mark candles. Hyperliquid uses its recorded trade candle extremes as mark proxies for maintenance and the matched spot market for settlement price proxies. The pair replay assumes bar-open fills. Actual contemporaneous two-venue book depth, order latency, realized oracle prices on Hyperliquid, deposits, and account-specific ADL are outside the data. This measures 28-day holding economics with hourly funding, without a claim about a millisecond trading advantage. [Binance market data API](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) · [Hyperliquid funding rules](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding)

```bash
python -m lane_z.spot_carry --capture --data data/agent-spot-carry --output data/agent-spot-carry/replay.json
python -m lane_z.spot_carry --capture --older-confirmation --data data/agent-spot-carry/older --output data/agent-spot-carry/older/replay.json
python -m lane_z.cross_venue_carry --capture
```

The first two commands prepare the Hyperliquid caches and can be skipped if they already exist. The final command writes `data/agent-venue-carry/results.json` containing the two fee variants, the per-venue ledgers, the funding forecasts, and the input hashes.
