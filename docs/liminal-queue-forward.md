# Prospective limUSD queue comparison

This separate paper cohort compares two exit policies using the same entry block, exact limUSD share count and 2,000 USDC-equivalent capital. It was specified on November 24, 2026, after the seven-day historical queue screen and before opening this new cohort. It does not change the November 23 instant-redemption track or count toward that track's streak. [Historical queue evidence](liminal-queued.md) · [Primary forward protocol](liminal-forward.md)

1. Record the first canonical block at or after the fixed launch timestamp once both RPC heads are at least eight blocks ahead. Require dRPC and PublicNode to agree on block, position size and quote. Apply the established 3 USDC gas reserve and 5 bp entry cost to both policies before using `previewDeposit` for the full amount. No subsequent rebalancing.
2. Fix the comparison deadline at entry block time plus 63 days. Simulate the standard redemption request at the first verified block at or after day 56. Mark the shares as unavailable for the instant route in this arm from request onward. Verify standard share size and pause conditions, and evaluate the lowest gross USDC quote for exact held shares at the fixed daily marks from request through deadline. Apply the 10 bp port-of-exit cost assumption and the deployed standard fee if it changes. Cash in the queue earns zero. The instant control uses the verified exact-share `previewRedeem` at day 63, current fee already incorporated, and the same 10 bp assumption.
3. Require direct chain evidence for standard capacity during the seven-day exit window. At least one complete, unambiguous single-request episode admitted after request time and fully paid by deadline must transfer at least 2,050 native USDC to the comparable owner. Verify the native transfer in the transaction receipt and account for partial fills and pre-existing or overlapping requests. A successful payment to someone else shows observed throughput. Settlement of our paper shares remains unknown. Report `capacity_supported` and conditional payout independently. An unverified quote, unmatched comparable, or blocked request fails the queue capacity gate at the deadline. There is no instant fallback for escrowed paper shares.
4. Compare conditional queued profit, the separately validated instant control, and 4% compounded cash on the entire 2,000 USDC over the actual 63-day cohort. Also report an extra 100 bp haircut. A winning quote or capacity sample before that date cannot score. Both arms use public read-only data.
