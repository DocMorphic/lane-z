# Hyperliquid selective funding carry above a cash hurdle

The earlier BTC and ETH hedge made paper profits, and the cash benchmark usually earned more. This test adds Unit Solana and native HYPE and screens the projected premium against the cost of committing the whole sleeve. Futures carry can reflect speculative demand and limited arbitrage capital. Perpetual funding is variable and the contract has no fixed convergence date. [Prior Lane Z results](spot-carry.md) · [BIS crypto carry](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry) · [Fundamentals of perpetual futures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888)

## Frozen protocol

- Use four exact Hyperliquid USDC spot pairs, with the native linear perpetual short against equal spot units. The HYPE perpetual oracle is USDC-denominated. BTC, ETH and SOL use USDT-denominated oracle prices and settle in USDC. The conservative usable-from dates are December 9, 2024 for HYPE (`@107`), February 17, 2025 for Unit BTC (`@142`), March 31 for Unit ETH (`@151`), and May 19 for Unit SOL (`@156`). These dates follow the later relevant recording availability and public launch evidence. A candidate needs 28 days of completed candles and hourly funding events after its usable-from date. Report the dated metadata evidence and actual source coverage for each asset. [Public spot metadata API](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/spot#retrieve-spot-metadata) · [Archive market metadata](https://api.tardis.dev/v1/exchanges/hyperliquid) · [Unit assets](https://docs.hyperunit.xyz/unit/about-unit/supported-assets/) · [Hyperliquid launch announcements](https://t.me/s/hyperliquid_announcements?before=381) · [Contract specifications](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/contract-specifications)
- Start with 2,000 USDC divided into two sleeves. Every 28 days at 00:00 UTC, estimate the next 28-day funding as four times the median of the four disjoint trailing seven-day sums. Use settled hourly rates strictly before the decision. Reduce positive rates by 25% and value them at a 0.5% lower oracle proxy. Raise the proxy by 0.5% for negative rates. This limits the influence of a brief spike and penalizes optimistic funding. Require at least 100,000 USDC of median four-hour traded notional in both legs over the trailing 28 days and an absolute spot/perp basis of at most 25 bps at the prior completed close.
- Withdraw from the funding forecast 23 bps of Tier 0 fees, 40 bps of stressed price allowance for all four fills, and a 25 bp adverse basis reserve, expressed on matched notional. Multiply the remaining edge by the 45% spot allocation. A sleeve's forecast must exceed the compounded 4% cash return over a full 28 days. Assign the two highest eligible assets one equal sleeve each, with remaining sleeves idle. Rank breaks ties alphabetically. Use the next four-hour open as the execution proxy and the previous completed closes for size. Fund the spot purchase outright and require separate initial USDC collateral at least equal to the matched short notional. Round to the coarser of the two lot sizes. Close at the next 28-day boundary, book all costs, and size each new vintage using the prior closing equity. Cash earns no yield. [Tier 0 fee schedule](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) · [Collateral rules](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/margining)
- Replay actual hourly funding through each position and the change in spot/perpetual basis. The main funding and execution stress uses 10 bps per fill, the 25% positive-rate haircut, and the adverse four-hour spot low/high with another 50 bps oracle-price allowance. The base uses 2 bps per fill and the recorded spot bar open as the oracle proxy. Funding rates are the observed settlements. The actual oracle values and synchronized trade prices are absent from the tape. Check margin using the worst perpetual price in each bar plus negative funding and a 5% maintenance floor, and flag a position if modeled size exceeds 1% of either entry/exit bar's reported volume. These bar-level tests cannot verify individual execution. Charge one additional 100 bp perpetual close penalty per open short and a 1% USD mark-down to terminal USDC as separate tail sensitivities. Account-specific auto-deleveraging and actual price moves after a broken hedge are outside the model. [Funding rate and cash-flow mechanics](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding) · [Auto-deleveraging](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/auto-deleveraging)
- Report actual P&L components, unallocated capital, modeled margin headroom, and the fixed 4% compounded cash comparator on all capital. Also replay an equal-weight, always-on matched hedge in all otherwise liquid and eligible pairs at each vintage, using the same costs and point-in-time data. Qualification requires the selective policy to beat compounded cash under the combined funding and execution stress in both consecutive untouched periods, with complete modeled exposures. The forced-exit and stablecoin shock sensitivities are reported separately.

## Dates and comparisons

Run the fixed schedule every 28 days beginning March 10, 2025, with data from February 10 where available. March 10–June 2 is descriptive. The two untouched primary tests are June 2–June 30 and June 30–July 28, 2025. Each ends before the old BTC and ETH study's August 1, 2025 warmup. Continue to November 22, 2026 as a later diagnostic. The BTC and ETH inputs and outcomes overlap prior research starting in August 2025 and are labeled retrospective in that run. The final truncated diagnostic liquidates by November 22, retaining the same 28-day decision hurdle. These settings were chosen before inspecting outcomes from any of the newly requested historical tapes.

## Native HYPE staking sensitivity

The staking candidate exclusively uses native HYPE/USDC spot and HYPE-USD perpetuals. Start at 2,000 USDC, use one fully funded 45%-notional sleeve, and hedge from the next four-hour bar open. Close both legs at the end of each of two consecutive 56-day periods, August 2–September 27 and September 27–November 22, 2026 UTC. Reinvest the previous ending balance at the next entry and pay both sides for each vintage. This diagnostic protocol was set after the June–July 2025 results and before inspecting the later carry outcomes. Staking was already live by May 2025, the spot and perpetual markets use USDC, and Tier 0 taker fees apply because the sleeve is unstaked at both endpoints. [May 2025 exchange announcement](https://t.me/s/hyperliquid_announcements/362) · [Contract specification](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/contract-specifications) · [Fee assessment and staking discounts](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees#staking-tiers)

Use the same stressed four-fill allowance and funding assumptions as selective carry. Assume delegation at entry and start the staking withdrawal queue seven days and four hours before the scheduled liquidation. Apply the exchange's illustrative 2.37% annual token reward for 400 million HYPE staked, with no validator commission, compounded in the token over the delegated interval. Price rewards at the planned exit less taker fees and slippage. The residual reward position is unhedged. This is a sensitivity because realized validator-by-validator rewards and actual queue completion are absent from the data. Show the break-even reward APY required to beat 4% cash at that exit, and the effect of a further 100 bp perpetual buyback penalty when the spot hedge may still be locked. The recovery gap after an unscheduled hedge break depends on the actual queue and the path of HYPE prices. [How to stake HYPE](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/onboarding/how-to-stake-hype) · [Staking rewards and withdrawal queue](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hypercore/staking)

HyperCore portfolio margin is a separate capital and liquidation model. The December 23, 2025 mainnet pre-alpha required more than $5 million in master account volume. The June 25, 2026 beta announcement expanded limits, and the current rules require more than $10,000 in account value or more than $5 million of weighted volume. A standalone 2,000 USDC paper sleeve with zero qualifying history falls below both gates. Borrowing would add rates driven by venue utilization and availability caps. This study retains separate fully funded margin. [December 23 pre-alpha announcement](https://t.me/hyperliquid_announcements/494) · [June 25 beta announcement](https://t.me/hyperliquid_announcements/563) · [Portfolio margin rules and borrow formula](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/portfolio-margin)

## Results through November 22, 2026

The selective strategy failed the 4% cash hurdle in both untouched windows under combined stress. It placed one modeled HYPE hedge in 23 vintages, then held its entire balance as idle USDC for the 18 retrospective vintages. The 154.96-bp funding threshold comprises 23 bps of fees, 40 bps of stressed execution, 25 bps of basis reserve, and 66.96 bps for the 4% cash comparator on the whole sleeve. HYPE's 191.35-bp forecast at the one entry materially overestimated realized funding. The best eligible later forecast was 154.19 bps on July 28, 2025. All subsequent signals fell further below the same hurdle.

Unallocated capital means unused sleeves, with short collateral included in the allocated sleeves. The two untouched selective vintages compounded to **+0.122%** stressed and **+0.305%** base, against **+0.604%** cash. The always-on benchmark compounded to **+0.342%** stressed and **+1.014%** base in the same two periods. It cleared 4% cash in zero of the 18 later periods at either cost assumption. Its frequent 28-day rollover paid 18 full round trips per continuously eligible pair over those later periods.

From 2,000 USDC on March 10, 2025, selective carry ended at **2,002.43 USDC** stressed or **2,006.10 USDC** base. Always-on carry ended at **1,965.28 USDC** stressed or **2,080.29 USDC** base. Compounded cash ended at **2,138.24 USDC** across that same horizon. No modeled exposure failed the initial collateral, 5% maintenance, or 1%-of-bar-volume checks. The one selective short's minimum modeled margin headroom was 369.29 USDC. Another 100 bp on its buyback changes the +2.43 USDC profit to a **−2.81 USDC** loss. A separate 1% terminal USDC mark-down changes it to a **−17.59 USD** loss against the initial dollar value. Both are sensitivities.
