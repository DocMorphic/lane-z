# JitoSOL staking with a Hyperliquid SOL hedge

## Frozen research protocol

JitoSOL accumulates Solana staking and MEV rewards in the redeemable SOL per share. Use direct minting with native SOL and direct redemption into a delayed stake account, with a native Hyperliquid SOL perpetual short to hedge the SOL units. Jito publishes timestamped historical conversion ratios and documents an ordinary direct redemption fee of 10 basis points and a delay of about one epoch. A Jito technical memorandum gives a two-to-five-day range. The stake pool is exposed to validator performance and smart contract failures. The hedge also introduces signed funding, separate liquidation risk, and a cross-chain transfer dependency. [Jito ratio API](https://www.jito.network/docs/jitosol/jitosol-liquid-staking/for-developers/stake-pool-api/#9-jitosolsol-ratio) · [Jito technical FAQs](https://www.jito.network/docs/jitosol/faqs/technical-faqs/) · [Jito withdrawal routes](https://www.jito.network/docs/jitosol/get-started/unstaking-jitosol-flow/unstaking-overview/) · [Jito memorandum](https://www.jito.network/JitoSOL-Staking-Tax-Memorandum.pdf)

- Freeze four chronological windows. Development runs August 1–December 1, 2025. Holdouts run December 1, 2025–April 1, 2026, April 1–August 1, 2026, and August 1–November 21, 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Reset the paper account to 3,000 USDC in each window. Use 45% for the spot leg and up to 55% for independently funded perp collateral. If the bought spot costs less than 45%, leave the remainder in idle USDC. The stake, in-transit SOL and withdrawal receipt are unavailable for margin. Use observed Hyperliquid Unit SOL against USDC (`@156`) and native SOL four-hour candles with actual hourly native funding. The Jito ratio is a separate source and outcome from the other shared-tape tests.
- Buy Unit SOL and short one native SOL perpetual unit per spot unit at the next four-hour open, sized using the larger of the last completed SOL prices and the maximum allowed trade costs in that run. Round down to the market lot and require both fills to clear the venue's minimum and remain at most 1% of the corresponding completed bar volume by size. Move the spot SOL to the native chain and mint after one additional bar. Use the last Jito pool-rate observation strictly before each decision or mint/burn proxy. Require rate age no more than 96 hours. The native SOL mint is modeled at the pool ratio with the route reserve below covering network and conversion friction.
- Request redemption three days and one four-hour bridge allowance before the fold ends, deduct 10 bps of redeemable SOL at the request and fix the net SOL quantity from there. Treat the no-yield withdrawal claim as received after three days and bridge it back to Unit SOL, then close both legs at the fold end. The perp stays open, fully funded, and accrues settlements through the handoffs. Charge 0.01 SOL before mint and 0.01 SOL after the modeled redemption as total reserves for each leg of cross-chain, priority, and conversion costs. Treat a failed or subminimum modeled bridge transfer as a failed fold. Documented current deposit minimum is 0.12 SOL. No historical network-fee or realized settlement tape is available. [Hyperliquid Solana deposits and current minimum](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/support/faq/deposit-or-transfer-issues-missing-lost/deposited-via-solana-network) · [Jito direct mint](https://www.jito.network/docs/jitosol/get-started/stake-sol-for-jitosol-flow/overview/)
- At Monday 00:00 UTC while the spot leg is staked, target one perp short unit per redeemable SOL using the last ratio and completed price. Apply the same decision immediately when fixing the no-yield withdrawal claim, if a rebalance is needed. Apply new targets after the run's lag and skip changes below the 10 USDC floor at the modeled open. Opening, closing and adjustments pay 4.5 bps Hyperliquid perpetual taker or 7 bps regular spot taker, plus 5 bps adverse allowance on each executed side. Use the less favorable hourly funding cash flow at change boundaries, and the contemporaneous 4h spot open as funding oracle proxy. Maintain at least 5% adverse sampled short notional in the independent hedge sleeve, allowing for funding debits and each bar high. Mark adverse portfolio impact with a joint price envelope for both legs and mark the entire account in USDC. Record stake accretion, venue basis, residual exposure, bridge reserves, funding and fees separately. [Hyperliquid fees](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) · [Hyperliquid funding](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding)
- Combined stress uses two bars of trade and deposit/return lag, a five-day redemption delay, 0.02 SOL on both bridge sides and 20 bps adverse allowance per transaction. Use each bar's low × 0.995 with 75% of credits retained, or high × 1.005 for debits, as funding price proxies. At the final boundary only the open is available. Include a separate 50 bp haircut of terminal stake-side proceeds to represent additional adverse route and SOL-basis execution. Diagnostic unstaked carry uses the same buy and short without stake, burn, transfer or related haircuts.
- Success requires beating the 4% effective annual compounded cash return on the whole account in each of the three consecutive holdouts at baseline and combined stress, with each hedge sleeve passing the collateral and margin checks. Cash itself is only an external benchmark. No account funds, keys, transactions or signed requests are needed.

### Execution cost update before scoring

Unit's public fee estimate returned a current SOL withdrawal fee of 0.000008 SOL and deposit fee of 0.017506612 SOL, with estimated times of four minutes and one minute respectively. The advertised deposit fee exceeded the provisional 0.01 SOL budget. Increase the return-side reserve to 0.03 SOL at baseline and 0.06 SOL under combined stress. Keep the outbound reserves of 0.01 and 0.02 SOL for network and minting costs. The return-side bridge allowance is one bar at baseline and two under stress. The SOL spot buy is capped at its 45% cash sleeve if the opening proxy exceeds the prior price used to size it. The matched perp size uses the capped modeled spot fill. This captures a fixed spending cap without permitting either sleeve to borrow. No ratio values, returns, or paper P&L had been examined when this change was made. ## Paper results and retirement

The protocol was frozen November 23, 2026 at 11:20:12 UTC, with the execution-cost update frozen at 11:21:07 UTC before scoring. The final SHA-256 was `76c3891b9deda3f1a0da16f43db5775c17b5eb3cbf51e9bc9178e694ecd60c68`. The documented ratio POST returned 484 observations from July 25, 2025 at 23:10:11 UTC to November 20, 2026 at 23:11:26 UTC. The longest gap was 25.50 hours. All required Hyperliquid spot, perpetual and signed funding observations were available. Historical Unit route quotes and individual bridge and redemption settlement times remain unverified and are modeled as specified.

The frozen price proxy rejected the August 1 Unit SOL sell at 1.91–1.93% of previous four-hour traded volume. It also rejected the Unit SOL buy in the following fold at 2.17–4.48%. The 5% participation column is a post-hoc capacity sensitivity. It makes all four windows measurable at the same modeled times without support from historical executable quotes. The tested route failed to beat cash in every stressed holdout even under that additional fill assumption.

The 5% baseline sensitivity booked $20.54, $22.62, and $25.56 of staking rewards across the three holdouts. Signed short funding contributed −$9.08, +$8.04, and +$38.34. Before costs, matched price and residual exposure contributed −$5.50, −$2.19, and +$1.06. The unstaked spot–perp control in the same sensitivity earned −$14.65, +$0.33, and +$30.25 at baseline and −$25.47, −$13.15, and +$11.78 under stress. The incremental reward from staking survived route costs in that comparison but remained too small relative to the full capital hurdle and funding risk.

No completed run breached separately funded perp margin. Minimum adverse hedge headroom was $585.85, the smallest fill was $10.04, and the maximum sampled residual SOL exposure was $12.93. Worst combined drawdown using contemporaneous opening-price marks was $32.65. The conservative intrabar envelope was up to $368.95 across all JitoSOL runs, from applying the SOL spot low and perp high together even when they may have occurred at different times. A separate Decimal replay checked 32 input hashes, 24 completed ledgers, eight liquidity rejections and 126 recorded fills across the frozen result and sensitivity. The modeled redemption uses the quoted pool ratio and the published direct withdrawal fee with an assumed release delay. It did not route any transactions.
