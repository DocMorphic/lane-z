# Hyperliquid funding-conditioned shock reversal

## Frozen research protocol

A temporary liquidity shortage could cause a large price move against an established one-sided derivatives position. Stefan Nagel links equity reversal returns to the compensation for providing liquidity during stressed markets. Maik Schmeling, Andreas Schrimpf, and Karamfil Todorov find that crypto carry rises with leveraged upside demand and predicts crashes in BTC and ETH. Hyperliquid also documents that its liquidation engine normally sends market orders to its order book. This experiment infers crowding from settled funding and tests a reversal after the initial shock. It has no liquidation or open-interest observations and cannot attribute the move to forced exits. [Nagel, *Evaporating Liquidity*](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w17653/w17653.pdf) · [BIS, *Crypto carry*](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry) · [Hyperliquid liquidation mechanics](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/liquidations)

- Freeze BTC, ETH, and SOL native perpetuals, with one-hour UTC candles starting May 4, 2026. Reset the entire 3,000 USDC paper account in each window. Development is May 8–July 13, first holdout July 13–September 14, and confirmation September 14–November 21, all 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Endpoints permit exiting positions. No prior decisions from these windows enter any fold. Feature warmup from earlier closed data is allowed. The selection mechanism is new, and some dates overlap other project tests.
- At decision hour `t`, calculate the log return from the close of the hour beginning at `t−2h` to the close of the hour beginning at `t−1h`. It must reach 0.01 in absolute terms and 2.5 times the sample standard deviation of the preceding 72 non-overlapping hourly log returns, with the shock excluded. Require a signed funding sum of at least 0.0003 in absolute terms over eight settlements `t−9h` through `t−2h`, inclusive, with a sign opposite the shock. Long after a qualifying drop and short after a qualifying increase. Rank simultaneous signals by largest absolute volatility-standardized shock, with alphabetical ticker tie-break. Allow one position or pending entry per account. A fully closed account may schedule a new entry in the same hour. The shock-only benchmark uses the same settings with the funding filter disabled.
- Set position magnitude to 50% of the full paper balance divided by the last known close, rounded down to the venue lot size. Buy or sell at the first trade proxy of the hour beginning one full hour after the decision. Hold exactly twelve hours from entry. Skip candidates whose modeled exit would go past the known fold end. Skip under-minimum entries and require full notional backing at entry. The stress run delays entry by two hours from decision while retaining the twelve-hour holding period. For the diagnostic long benchmark, allocate the same total 50% across the three native perpetuals once, enter with the same delay, and exit at the end of each window.
- Charge the normal 4.5 basis point taker fee and a 5 basis point adverse allowance per side. The timing sensitivity changes only the entry delay. The combined sensitivity uses the two-hour entry delay, 15 basis points per side and adverse funding valuation. Apply the actual hourly settled rate against traded opening price as the funding oracle proxy. The combined sensitivity uses each completed bar's low × 0.995 to value credits, with only 75% of those credits retained, and the high × 1.005 for debits. Use the endpoint open for settlements coinciding with exiting an asset. At any transaction hour charge the less favorable of its pre- and post-trade funding, allowing credits only through an unchanged position.
- Mark current holdings at every open. Sample adverse price moves each hour using every long's traded low and every short's traded high for drawdown and 5% maintenance coverage. Track actual modeled quantity, fees, dollar funding, turnover, execution allowance, adverse marks and uninvested equity. A maintenance breach or unbacked entry fails the trial. OHLCV trade prices and the explicit execution allowance are proxies for quotes, market depth, and funding oracle prices.
- Use a 4% effective annual compounded return on the full account as the hurdle. The funding-qualified rule must beat this hurdle in both consecutive holdouts under baseline and combined stress. Diagnose the funding screen against the shock-only rule. Report eligible and executed counts and limit inference from sparse or concentrated evidence. Use the same frozen parameters in every fold. No credentials, order routing, or real capital are involved. ## Paper results and retirement

The protocol above was frozen before the candle capture at 11:11:21 UTC on November 23, 2026. Its SHA-256 at freeze was `d42204e7882e8e2bfefc1a2b6d55f3587eb7fcb6f4458b33e8b7f3ac772d3186`.

The 3 basis point funding threshold occurred at 18, 3, and 5 asset-hours across the windows before applying the shock screen. The largest sign-aligned funding sums immediately preceding qualifying shocks were 1.79, 2.78, and 1.44 basis points. The funding screen was inactive in all three windows and missed both cash hurdles. Unconditional reversal lost $76.14 on price alone in the first holdout and made $215.46 on price in the second. At the two-hour delay the second holdout price contribution dropped to −$21.55. Retire both fixed rules. Evidence from the funding screen is limited to inactivity at the specified threshold. The holdouts do not test the profitability of reversals after observed forced liquidations.

The 50% funded equal-long benchmark earned −$282.62 in development, +$394.64 in the first holdout, and −$469.33 in confirmation at baseline assumptions. Maximum adverse drawdowns were $392.92 for any shock-only run and $719.23 for any equal-long run. Minimum adverse maintenance headroom across all runs was $2,448.12, and all modeled legs cleared the $10 notional floor. Independent arithmetic from the raw tapes confirmed 83 file hashes, all selected shock times, 414 complete asset round trips across runs, signed funding, lots, and fee and margin accounting. The smallest modeled fill before the adverse allowance was $327.46.
